Songdo OK Channel Manager

Internal tool of Songdo OK Real Estate Office

Songdo OK Channel Manager is an internal tool that Songdo OK Real Estate Office uses to manage our own YouTube channel, 송도OK부동산, which shares information about apartment complexes, local news and daily life in Songdo International City, Incheon. The same information is published on our blog.

What it does

  • Uploads YouTube Shorts made from our own blog posts, with title, description, tags, thumbnail and schedule
  • Updates metadata and captions of our own videos
  • Reads analytics of our own channel to plan content

The tool uses YouTube API Services. It is used only by our team, is not offered to the public, and never accesses other channels' data.
